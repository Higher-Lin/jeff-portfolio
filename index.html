01 · POSITIONING

From engineering details to content people can use.

A connector mechanism, a current-balancing concept, a booth filled with products. Each needs a clear visual explanation. At Bellwether, that starts with PMs and engineers: checking the structure, understanding the application and deciding what the audience needs to see.

The production work follows — scripts, storyboards, 3D, motion, live-action footage and post-production. For outsourced films, the scope also covers briefs, proposal and quotation comparison, consolidated feedback, technical reviews and delivery coordination.

Earlier work at MPC Canada and MoonShine Animation brings another layer: procedural FX, international production reviews and LED virtual-production workflows. That technical background supports both the making of the content and the conversations needed to deliver it.
